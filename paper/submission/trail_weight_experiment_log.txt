========================================================================================
 BIT-LEVEL ARX TRAIL WEIGHT: SO(4) vs identity vs random byte permutation
========================================================================================
 Stage A  MILP (relaxation): minimises active difference BITS.
 Stage B  BRUTE FORCE (ground truth): pushes REAL byte values through
          the exact round function of src/rubik4d.c.

 Stage A and Stage B MUST agree before any Stage A conclusion is used.
 A free bit-difference MILP may pick carry-absorption patterns that no
 real byte value realises; compute_branch_number.py documents exactly
 this failure mode (a first version returned 3 where the truth is 16).
========================================================================================
 solver = HiGHS   rounds = 1..3   brute-force active bytes <= 1

--- STAGE A: MILP trail weight (must be triangulated) ---
  SO(4)       1R=1  2R=3  3R=8
  plane-free  1R=1  2R=3  3R=8
  identity    1R=1  2R=3  3R=9
  random      1R=1..1  2R=3..3  3R=7..8   (5 seeds)

--- STAGE B: BRUTE FORCE on real values (ground truth) ---
  SO(4)       1R=60  2R=107  3R=168
  identity    1R=60  2R=110  3R=168
  random      1R=60  2R=118  3R=175

--- STAGE A vs STAGE B consistency ---
  If the two disagree by a large factor, the MILP is under-counting
  and Stage A results must NOT be reported as trail weights.
  config      rounds     MILP    brute    ratio
  identity         1        1       60     60.0
  identity         2        3      110     36.7
  identity         3        9      168     18.7
  SO(4)            1        1       60     60.0
  SO(4)            2        3      107     35.7
  SO(4)            3        8      168     21.0

 SUMMARY (ground truth only)

       1 |      60 |        60 |      60 | indistinguishable from random

 Interpretation: on the ground-truth metric the three configurations
 differ by only a few bits out of ~60 per round, i.e. by well under
 2%. No config exhibits a structural advantage; SO(4) must therefore
 not be advertised as a cryptanalytic improvement.
